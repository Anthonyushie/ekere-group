## Direction contract

THESIS: The group's own name, carved. A dark hardwood beam cut into fixed registers, one per venture, each incised with its true stage. It refuses the arrangement this category always ships — a dimmed aerial hero over a centred headline, three equal division cards, a fabricated statistics strip — because every one of those parts requires a claim this company cannot honestly make.

OWN-WORLD: Ibibio/Efik carved wood percussion. Ground #15100C iroko with a 1px/7px vertical incision grain at .055 alpha, soft-light blended. Type in #E9E2D3 sapwood. #B8863C struck brass for live status and the primary action; #8C2F1E oxblood for what is not yet true; #8F8475 ash for apparatus. Dividers are 1px grooves that fade at both ends, plus one incised chevron band (18×9px, two 135°/225° gradients, .62 opacity) used only between major registers. Big Shoulders Display 700 for display, Archivo for text, Martian Mono for status and apparatus. Brass plate buttons carry a 2px solid underlip and a deep soft shadow — cut metal seated into wood, never a floating card. No cards anywhere, no kickers, no gradient text, no glass.

STORY: The visitor understands in one viewport that this is a Nigerian corporate group with three declared ventures. They come to believe it because it tells them, unprompted, which ventures are not running — the one thing a shell company never does. They open a conversation.
