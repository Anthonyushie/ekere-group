Ekere Group

Notice of Formation
and Statement of Intent

Being a declaration of the ventures of the group, the stage each has presently reached, and an invitation to those who would partner in them.

Article I — Community Education

Presently active

Ekere Group holds that informed communities are better equipped to protect their own wellbeing. The group's first initiative is a programme of school outreach on the effects of drug abuse upon the brain, and upon the decisions a young person makes.

Sessions are in preparation with schools in Port Harcourt. Nothing is claimed as delivered that has not been delivered.

Article II — Property

In development

The group pursues property improvement and long-term development, with attention to identifying opportunity, enhancing what already stands, and holding for value rather than for turnover.

No completed development is presented here. Opportunities are under assessment, and enquiries from landholders and joint-venture partners are received.

Article III — Waste to Energy

Ambition only

The group holds a long-term ambition toward improved waste management in Port Harcourt, and toward the recovery of energy from waste that is presently burned or left standing.

No facility exists. No licence is held. The group seeks technical, research and development partners with whom the question may be properly studied.
