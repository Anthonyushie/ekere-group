## Platform

web

## Stack

Custom-coded front end, confirmed by the user: Next.js or Astro + Tailwind, deployed to Vercel or Netlify. The existing Envato "Consulten" WordPress/Elementor template is explicitly discarded — the user rejected a template refit on the grounds that it reads as a refitted consultancy template. Build is to be executed stage by stage in later Claude Code sessions, not in this one.

## Users

Primary: institutional partners and investors evaluating whether Ekere Group is a serious counterparty — development finance, banks, technical partners for waste-to-energy, joint-venture property partners. They arrive sceptical, often from a search or an introduction, and are looking for evidence of seriousness within seconds.

Secondary, confirmed as materially important: government bodies, councils and NGOs (Rivers State ministries, school boards, international NGOs) assessing legitimacy and public-good framing; and the Nigerian and diaspora public, for whom local credibility and recognition matter.

The confirmed priority is: design primarily for institutional partners, while keeping the community and environmental material strong enough to carry weight with government and NGOs.

## Product Purpose

Ekere Group is a parent company / corporate group based in Nigeria, with activity centred on Port Harcourt. The website exists to establish a credible public identity for the group at a stage when most of its ventures are intentions rather than operations, and to open conversations with partners.

Success is a partner, institution or official finishing the site believing Ekere Group is a serious, honest organisation worth a reply — and sending that reply.

## Positioning

A purpose-driven parent company: the group's stated reason for existing is that enterprise should create long-term value without damaging people's health and livelihoods. Its distinguishing position is honesty about stage — it publishes what it is building, what it is developing, and what it aspires to, as three separate things. A competitor cannot truthfully copy that because most early-stage groups present ambitions as operations.

## Operating Context

- Base of operations: Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria.
- Three declared areas of focus:
  1. **Real Estate & Property Development** — property improvement, renovation and long-term development. A current business interest.
  2. **Environmental Innovation** — a long-term vision for improved waste management and waste-to-energy in Port Harcourt. A development vision, explicitly NOT an existing facility.
  3. **Community Education & Social Impact** — educational outreach, with a featured school drug-awareness initiative teaching students the effects of drug abuse on the brain and the value of informed decisions. The priority initiative.
- Visitors will often be on mobile, on Nigerian mobile networks. Page weight and load behaviour are a credibility factor, not just a performance metric.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site must distinguish three states for every venture: operating, in development, aspiration. This is a hard content rule, not a stylistic preference.
- Nothing may be fabricated: no statistics, no completed-project counts, no client or partner logos, no testimonials, no pricing, no team roster, no awards, no press. These were all present in the discarded template and must not reappear.
- The six-page structure is confirmed: Home, About Us, Our Ventures, Our Impact, News & Insights, Contact.
- News & Insights ships only if there is genuine content; otherwise it is built but unpublished and kept out of the navigation.
- A working contact form with an enquiry-type field is required for launch. Enquiry types: General, Business Opportunity, Partnership Proposal, Community Initiative, Environmental Project, Other.
- Privacy Policy and Terms of Use must reflect actual data collection.
- **Undecided / unverified:** legal registration details, official postal address, verified telephone number, official email address, social accounts, founder name and title, domain arrangement for ekeregroup.org, whether individual venture sub-pages are needed at launch. None of these may be invented; all must ship as explicit placeholders the user fills in.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Ekere Group**. Spelled and cased exactly this way.
- Existing descriptor in use: "Building with Purpose".
- Existing domain: ekeregroup.org.
- Voice: measured, plain, institutional. British English spelling (recognise, programme, organisation) as used throughout the user's own content.
- No visual constraints were volunteered by the user, and none are recorded here.

## Evidence on Hand

- Full draft copy for the homepage, About Us, the three venture areas and the contact page, written by the user and approved as the content foundation.
- SEO page titles and the homepage meta description, drafted.
- **Photography and imagery: none.** The user confirmed they have essentially no usable photographs — no property photos, no Port Harcourt location photography, no founder portrait, no school outreach images. The design must look deliberate and expensive without photography, and must not be built around image slots that will sit empty or be filled with stock. Any imagery added later is an upgrade, not a dependency.
- No logo asset confirmed. Wordmark must be typographic and work with no logo file.
- No metrics, no track record, no named partners, no completed projects.

## Product Principles

1. **Stage honesty is the brand.** Every venture is labelled with its real status. The credibility this buys is the group's only real asset at launch, and the design must make the labelling feel like confidence rather than apology.
2. **Structure stands in for track record.** With no photography, no metrics and no logos, the evidence of seriousness has to come from rigour — typography, grid, hierarchy, precision, restraint, and copy that says exactly what it means.
3. **Partners lead, the public-good case follows immediately.** The group's social and environmental intent is not a footnote to be reached after three scrolls; it is part of the opening argument.
4. **Nothing on the page may be unfillable.** Every slot the design creates must be fillable from what the group actually has today, with placeholders that are obviously placeholders where facts are missing.
5. **Weight matters.** The site must be fast on a Nigerian mobile connection; a slow site undermines every claim of competence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA is the target: this is a site that government bodies and institutional partners will open. Accessible heading order, real text rather than text-in-images, visible focus states, and full keyboard operability of the navigation and contact form.
